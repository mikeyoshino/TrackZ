# Contact-sheet source/index

The embedded contact sheet contains all 90 dedicated PNGs as a review aid for the product owner/anatomy reviewer. It does not approve artwork and cannot change any record from `Draft`.

Use `catalog.json` order as the contact-sheet order: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs, then Core; fifteen cards per body part. The sheet is laid out left-to-right in ten columns and then top-to-bottom. The source filename for each card is its `imagePath` in the manifest.
